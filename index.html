Wema Bank Staff Multipurpose Cooperative Society

Registered as a Cooperative Multipurpose Society (CMS) in 1993 with two hundred pioneering members, the Society has continued to wax stronger over the years — growing savings, disbursing loans and acquiring property for the benefit of Wema Bank staff nationwide.

Est. 1993 Staff-Owned Not-for-Profit
Who we are

A cooperative built by staff, for staff.

Every naira contributed is a naira working for a colleague — and, in time, for you.

WEMACOOP pools the monthly contributions of Wema Bank employees into a shared fund. That fund is lent back to members at fair rates, invested in property for staff housing, and returned as dividends — all governed by members, for members.

Mutual well-being

Improving members' financial well-being has been the Society's founding purpose since 1993.

Real property, real returns

From Purple Villa, Alakuko to serviced flats in Somolu — member contributions become bricks and mortar.

Member governance

Run by a Board of Trustees elected from within the Society, accountable to every member.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Who can join WEMACOOP?+

Any confirmed staff of Wema Bank Plc is eligible to apply for membership, subject to Board approval.

How is my monthly contribution deducted?+

Contributions are deducted directly from your salary through payroll each month, so you never have to remember to save.

When can I apply for a loan?+

Members become eligible for loans after meeting the minimum contribution period set by the Society's by-laws, currently reviewed by the Board.

How are dividends calculated?+

Dividends are declared annually from the Society's surplus, in proportion to each member's shareholding, and approved at the Annual General Meeting.

Can I exit the Society and withdraw my savings?+

Yes. Members who leave the Bank's employment or resign membership can apply for withdrawal, processed according to the Society's exit policy.
